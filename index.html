How to Use

  1. Install AltStore on your iOS device.
  2. Tap one of the "Add Source" buttons above, or copy the URL below and paste it into AltStore's Sources tab.
  3. Browse and install apps directly from AltStore.

Classic requires AltServer running on a Mac or PC.
PAL is available in the EU, Japan, and Brazil on iOS 18.0+.
